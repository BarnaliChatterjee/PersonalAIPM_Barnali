My product philosophy.

The strongest PMs connect customer needs, business strategy, technology constraints and execution — without losing sight of the outcome.

Start with the problem

Frame the user pain, business opportunity and evidence before jumping to features.

Make complexity usable

Especially in banking, great product work means making regulation, workflows and technology feel simple to the end user.

Measure the outcome

Adoption, efficiency, conversion, revenue, quality and time-to-value matter more than feature count.
